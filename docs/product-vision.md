# Product vision

Help learners understand what to study, why prerequisites matter, and what to review
through a visual mastery roadmap that supplements a Discrete Mathematics course.

## Available today

Thirteen standalone maps with module-local practice and browser-local mastery.
Their content and interfaces remain prototypes requiring systematic review.
The shared topic guide connects the available maps. Proof Methods & Induction
also saves ungraded written drafts locally, separately from quiz progress.

All thirteen planned maps are available, including Sequences & Recurrences and
Discrete Probability. Complete the [curriculum repair handoff](curriculum-roadmap.md#remaining-task-handoff)
before integrating them into a unified platform; map availability does not mean
the curriculum or platform is complete.

## Planned experience

Connect the topic worlds into a shared knowledge graph. Add 15–30 minute Daily Quests,
accounts and synchronized mastery, a personal mistake collection, spaced and targeted
review, weak-topic detection, adaptive practice, and progress visualization.

## Architecture direction

Keep content and interactions together while developing the curriculum. Once repeated
patterns are understood, define stable node and question IDs, prerequisites, lessons,
visualizations, labs, feedback, mastery evidence, and recommendation rules. Then extract
shared content schemas and UI components and build the unified application.

Framework, backend, database, hosting, and account-provider choices remain undecided.
Preserve node IDs and plan progress migration before changing storage keys or origins.
