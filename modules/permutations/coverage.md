# Permutations coverage and review

Reviewed 2026-09-22 against the supplied 22-page Permutations PDF. This is a source-aligned
prototype; it is not a verified syllabus or a guarantee of exam coverage.

## Included

The 25 nodes cover the permutation/bijection bridge; arrangements; orbits, fixed points and orbit
partitions; equivalent notation and factorial counting; composition and noncommutativity;
transpositions and parity; inverse cycles and products; Japanese ladders; combing; inversions;
and the minimum-rung/parity connection. All 100 answer keys and explanations were checked.

## Remaining depth

Practice is multiple choice and emphasizes recall and recognition. The PDF multiplication tables,
long computations, written-proof Exercises 5–7, and ladder construction Exercises 8–10 are not
reproduced as equivalent assessed work. The interactive ladder builder has five posts, so it cannot
construct the seven-post Exercise 10. Written proof production and durable retention are not assessed.

Each node has a four-question pool and displays all four questions. Mastery therefore requires 4/4;
3/4 is partial. Recommendations are module-local.
