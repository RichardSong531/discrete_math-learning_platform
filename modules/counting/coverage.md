## Remaining depth

The questions emphasize recognition, model selection, and short computations. The
map does not reproduce all 35 source exercises or assess complete written proofs.
Straight and straight-flush counters, the eight-boy/eight-girl pairing exercise, and
the ternary-string exercise requiring no occurrences of digit 1 are not separately
assessed. Combinations with repetition includes a frequency interpretation, but no
full stars-and-bars proof. The source only briefly mentions countable sets; it does
not develop a substantive section on countability.

Each check uses its four-question pool; mastery requires 4/4 and 3/4 is partial.
Repeated exposure to that pool does not measure durable retention or independent
proof proficiency. Mixed Practice does not update individual node mastery.
