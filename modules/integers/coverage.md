# Integers coverage and review

Reviewed 2026-10-06 against the supplied 48-page *Properties Of Integers* PDF.
This records source alignment, not a verified syllabus or a guarantee of exam coverage.

## Included

All 34 nodes, 136 answer keys/explanations, and 48 glossary entries were reviewed.
The map covers the integer/well-ordering foundations; signed division and divisibility;
perfect numbers; parity; decimal digit rules; positional bases and conversions; primes,
unique factorization and Mersenne numbers; gcd, Bézout, lcm and Euclid; prime search;
factorials; floor/ceiling; and mixed synthesis.

The gcd node now follows basic divisibility, so learners can reach all nodes without
the original circular dependency through prime factorization and Euclid's lemma.
Navigation recommendations are local to this module.

## Remaining depth

Practice emphasizes multiple-choice recognition and short computations. The
irrational-square-root proof on page 23 and most long proofs/exercises are not covered
as assessed work. The map does not assess written-proof production or durable retention.

Each node displays its four-question pool, with shuffled option order. Mastery requires
4/4; 3/4 is partial. Repeating this small pool is not independent evidence of mastery.
Mixed Practice is review and does not update individual node mastery.
