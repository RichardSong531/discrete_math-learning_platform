## Included

All 37 nodes, 148 answer keys/explanations, and 44 glossary entries were reviewed.

and additive operations, matrix equations, triangular examples, multiplication and

## Remaining depth

The four questions per node emphasize recognition and short computations. Many long
computations and written-proof exercises remain outside these checks. Dedicated
lower-triangular lessons and product-closure proof practice from the page 31 exercises
are not included. General inverse construction, elimination, rank, eigenvalues, and
general n×n determinant algorithms are outside the current map. The inverse/determinant
relationship is summarized without an inverse-construction lab.

Mastery requires 4/4 on the small node pool; 3/4 is partial. Repeated exposure to the
same pool does not assess durable retention or written-proof proficiency. Mixed
Practice is review and does not change individual node mastery.
